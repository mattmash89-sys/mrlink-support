MR-LINK Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

MR-LINK does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics, from you or your device, with anyone — including us.

  • The app makes no network or internet connections of any kind.
  • All data (your saved configuration files) is stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage, and is never sent anywhere.
  • The app communicates only with the physical USB accessory you connect it to, directly, with nothing routed through us or any third party.

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated.
